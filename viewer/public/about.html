GTNH Seed Map

A seed and biome viewer with seed search for GT New Horizons worlds. It runs a Rust port of the pack's world generation in your browser.

Supported worlds

  • GT New Horizons 2.9 and 2.10, world types RWG2_CONTINENTS (the pack default) and RWG2. Pack data comes from 2.9.0, and the predictions are checked against real 2.9.0 and 2.10.0 worlds.
  • Not supported: worlds made before 2.9 that kept their old generator (Realistic World Gen 1), other world types, and modified worldgen configs.
  • Dimensions: Overworld, Nether, Twilight Forest and the End.
  • A new pack release can change generation. It is checked with a dump from the new pack before it is marked as supported.

What is exact and what is estimated

Exact means it was compared with real generated worlds with no mismatches. Estimated means the result depends on things a seed alone does not fix, such as the order chunks were loaded in, or on a model that is only partly certain. The map says “est.”, “~” or gives a percentage where it estimates.

FeatureAccuracy
BiomesExact, including the per-block biome you see in F3, Thaumcraft and Forestry.
Terrain and surface blocksExact when zoomed in, before trees and ores. Zoomed out, heights are a blend that is usually within 2 blocks.
Strongholds, villages, mineshaftsExact positions. Includes the stronghold portal room and village style. Village layouts are not predictable from the seed.
Spawn pointExact on every checked world.
Nether fortressesExact layout, blaze spawners, wart rooms and chest loot.
Nether, Twilight Forest and End terrainExact before decoration. Twilight Forest structures are exact.
HEE End islands and towersExact positions, island biomes and tower rooms.
GT underground fluidsExact (every checked chunk).
Bee climate, LootGames dungeonsExact.
GT ore veinsAbout 9 in 10. The vein type and height are exact, but whether a vein generates depends on which nearby chunk loaded first.
Monster rooms, chest carts, geodes, lakesUsually right. They depend on terrain and on what generated before them.
Roguelike dungeonsThe search ring is exact. The likely spot is an estimate.
Thaumcraft nodes and structuresUsually right. They depend on trees and on the order neighbouring chunks generated in.
Oil depositsAbout 50–80%. The popup gives the confidence.
Trees and plantsPlausible, not block-exact. Exact load order is only modelled around spawn.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Seeds, searches, markers and settings never leave your device. The page sends no analytics and sets no cookies. Saved views, markers and settings live in your browser's local storage.
